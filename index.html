Process

The beauty unfolds in my process

Let me introduce you to PRESH — pulled from my own name, because everything I build carries a piece of who I am in it.

P

Partner

I start by partnering with the people closest to the problem: researchers, institutions, community members. No strategy works if it's built without the people who'll actually live with it. This is where I listen before I design anything.

R

Research

I dig into what's already there before building something new. What standards exist, what's been tried, what data or context is missing. Good work builds on what's real, not what's assumed.

E

Engage

I bring people in early through workshops, mentorship and hands-on sessions. Change doesn't stick if it's handed down — it sticks when people are part of building it.

S

Sustain

I design for what lasts, not just what launches. That means aligning with open standards, thinking about interoperability, and building systems that can survive beyond me.

H

Hand off

At the heart of all I do is access. I document everything clearly so it can be picked up, replicated and carried forward by others — because the biggest barrier to progress usually isn't ability. It's access and mentorship.
